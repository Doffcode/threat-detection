# Abstract {.unnumbered}

This Phase 3 review presents a surveillance video analysis system that supports weapon localization, fight classification, and movement evidence review in one dashboard. The strongest verified result is from the frozen X3D-M fight classifier. At a decision threshold of 0.40, it correctly classified 273 of 300 balanced Surveillance Camera Fight Dataset (SCFD) clips, yielding 91.0% accuracy and 90.4% fight-class F1. The dashboard applies the same classifier to five-second windows, while the benchmark used one prediction per whole clip. The default weapon detector achieved 0.252 mean average precision at an overlap threshold of 0.50 on Innovatus CCTV frames. A separate detector-only tabletop validation achieved 0.919 under a different protocol. The movement branch provides timestamped evidence and replay. Its 24-clip review recorded zero alerts and matched zero of 15 labeled intervals, whose temporal validity remains unresolved. This report documents the implemented scope, the source artifacts for each metric, and the limits of the measured results.

**Keywords:** surveillance video, fight classification, weapon detection, movement evidence, human review
